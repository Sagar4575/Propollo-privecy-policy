1. Who Operates Propollo?

Propollo is currently operated by an individual and is not represented in this policy as a registered company.

Platform: Propollo

Website: https://propollo.online

Privacy and support contact: propollo.online@gmail.com

For privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, contact us using the email address above. Where applicable data protection law requires us to identify a specific legal controller or provide additional contact details, we will provide the relevant information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide Propollo's features, manage user accounts, maintain security, and operate the platform. The information we collect depends on how you use Propollo.

2.1 Information collected through Google sign-in

You can register for or sign in to Propollo using Google where that authentication option is available. When you use Google sign-in, we may receive information associated with your Google Account, including:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google profile picture.
  • A unique account identifier.
  • Authentication information and account metadata necessary to establish and maintain your Propollo account.

The information received depends on the Google authentication configuration and permissions used by Propollo. Propollo does not receive your Google Account password through the standard Google sign-in process. Google handles the authentication of your Google Account.

We use the information received to create or authenticate your Propollo account, associate your account with your activity on the platform, and provide account-related features.

Google operates independently and handles information according to its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Disconnecting Propollo from your Google Account does not automatically delete your Propollo account or information already stored by Propollo. To delete your Propollo account, follow Section 11 of this policy.

2.2 Information collected through email and password registration

If you register using an email address and password, we process the information necessary to create and maintain your account, which may include:

  • Your email address.
  • Your account identifier.
  • Authentication and session information.
  • Account verification and security information, where applicable.

Authentication credentials are handled through the authentication infrastructure used by Propollo. We do not intentionally collect or store your email account password as ordinary profile information. We may process account status and authentication-related records to maintain account security, prevent unauthorized access, and support account deletion.

2.3 Profile information

Depending on the features available, you may provide:

  • A display name or username.
  • A profile picture or avatar.
  • A short biography or other profile details.
  • Saved firms, favourites, or watchlist preferences.
  • Other information you voluntarily add to your profile.

Profile pictures may be obtained through Google sign-in or uploaded by you, depending on the available functionality. Some profile information and pictures may be publicly visible on Propollo. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public profile or uploaded picture.

2.4 Reviews and ratings

Propollo allows users to submit reviews and ratings where these features are available. When you submit a review or rating, we may process the content of your submission, the rating, associated account information, and relevant submission metadata.

Reviews and ratings may be displayed publicly and may be associated with your display name or other profile information, depending on the design of the relevant feature.

Please do not include passwords, financial account details, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information in reviews or ratings. You can request deletion of your reviews and ratings through the account-deletion process described in Section 11, subject to applicable legal requirements.

2.5 Technical and security information

When you visit or use Propollo, our hosting infrastructure and service providers may process technical information needed to deliver the website and maintain security. This may include IP addresses, timestamps, browser or device information, request logs, error reports, and information needed to detect suspicious activity.

The exact information collected depends on the production infrastructure and its configuration. Propollo does not currently intend to use analytics or advertising-tracking systems as part of its website analytics. However, essential authentication, security, hosting, and operational technologies may still process technical information. We will update this policy if our use of analytics or tracking changes materially.

2.6 Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact us at propollo.online@gmail.com, we may process your email address, message, attachments, and any information you choose to include.

We use this information to respond to your request, provide support, investigate problems, and handle privacy or account-related inquiries. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Propollo is an informational and comparison platform. It is not intended to collect your trading account credentials or process proprietary trading firm purchases on behalf of those firms.

Unless a specific feature is introduced and appropriately disclosed, Propollo does not request:

  • Your Google Account password.
  • Your MT4, MT5, cTrader, or other trading-platform password.
  • Your proprietary trading firm's account password.
  • Your banking password or payment card security credentials.
  • Government identity documents or KYC records for the purpose of registering with an external proprietary trading firm.

If you register with a proprietary trading firm through an external link, that firm may request personal information, payment information, identity documents, or trading credentials directly from you. Such information is handled by the external firm under its own privacy policy and terms. Propollo does not automatically receive the information you provide directly to that firm.

11. Account Deletion and Removal of Personal Information

Propollo provides an account-deletion option through the application's Settings. Account deletion is intended to remove your Propollo account and eligible associated personal information. It is not merely a sign-out or temporary deactivation.

11.1 How to delete your account

To request account deletion:

  1. Sign in to your Propollo account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Navigate to the account-management section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the consequences of deletion.
  6. Complete the verification and final confirmation steps shown by the application.
  7. Confirm the deletion request.

The application may require explicit confirmation to prevent accidental deletion. If you cannot access your account, contact propollo.online@gmail.com for assistance with an account-deletion request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before proceeding.

11.2 What deletion is intended to remove

Depending on the information associated with your account, the deletion process is intended to remove or appropriately handle:

  • Your account and profile information.
  • Account-linked authentication records.
  • Uploaded profile pictures and other account-linked files.
  • Saved firms, favourites, watchlists, and preferences.
  • Reviews and ratings submitted through your account.
  • Other account-linked user content and personal information eligible for deletion.

We also intend to revoke or invalidate access to the deleted account and prevent it from continuing to be used as an active Propollo account. The precise completion of these operations depends on the relevant database relationships, storage objects, authentication configuration, and other systems involved.

11.3 Deletion timing

When you confirm deletion through Settings, the application is intended to initiate the deletion process immediately. The account should no longer be treated as an active account once deletion has been successfully completed. However, initiating a deletion request does not necessarily mean that every associated record, backup copy, or third-party record is physically erased at the exact same moment. Some operations may require additional processing or provider-side handling. We will not claim that deletion is complete until the relevant process has actually succeeded.

11.4 Reviews and ratings

When you delete your account, your reviews and ratings are intended to be deleted along with your account-linked personal information. Where a review or rating is stored separately from the main account record, the deletion process must also handle the associated record. Any information that must lawfully be retained will be handled according to applicable legal requirements.

11.5 Backups and third-party systems

Propollo uses third-party infrastructure to store and process information. Some information may remain in protected backups for a limited period according to the relevant provider's backup-retention and recovery procedures. Where individual deletion from a backup is not immediately available, eligible information will be handled under the applicable retention and deletion procedures, with appropriate safeguards. Deleting a Propollo account does not automatically delete information held independently by Google or an external proprietary trading firm. Where a provider independently controls its information, you may need to submit a separate request to that provider.

11.6 Lawful exceptions

We may retain limited information when required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, address a dispute, establish or defend a legal claim, or protect the security of Propollo and its users. Any retained information should be limited to the purpose for which retention is necessary and kept only for the appropriate period.

11.7 Account recovery

Account deletion is intended to be permanent once completed. After deletion, you may lose access to your profile, saved firms, preferences, reviews, and other account-related features. We may be unable to restore the deleted account. If you wish to use Propollo again, you may need to register a new account.
